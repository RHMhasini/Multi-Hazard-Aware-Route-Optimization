Component 3 Research Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

In natural disasters such as monsoon floods and slope landslides in Ratnapura, shortest-distance paths frequently traverse hazardous valley bottlenecks. Component 3 integrates spatial hazard rasters directly with road graphs and formulates a bi-objective optimization problem to minimize both travel duration and hazard vulnerability.

Primary Edge Risk: R_e = max_{x ∈ e} M(x)
Cumulative Route Exposure: R(p) = ∑_{e ∈ p} \text{length}(e) × R_e
Bi-Objective Problem: min_{p ∈ P} [T(p), R(p)] \quad \text{subject to } R_e \le \tau_{\text{ceiling}}

The system implements both a Weighted-Sum Pareto Sweep and an exact Bi-Criteria Label-Setting Algorithm to discover non-dominated solutions, filtered with Pareto dominance checks. An explainable Utopian Knee-Point decision rule selects the operationally optimal compromise route for emergency dispatch (Component 4).
